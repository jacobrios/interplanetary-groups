Caught-up note

Orbit acknowledges the decline without arguing with it, then confirms there is nothing else. Plain words, no em-dashes, no exclamation.

  • Reuses the chat bubble (.gh-msgrow / .gh-msg) so the voice reads as the same Orbit from the feed.
  • It is the panel's whole content in this state. On collapse the strip unmounts if no standing yes remains.

Rules this region follows

  • Hierarchy. The pinned card is raised, bordered and shadowed and owns the only teal action on the screen. The strip and panel are flat surfaces with hairline rules, so the confirmed plan always outranks the maybes.
  • Teal. One primary per element. Nothing in this region takes teal, because the chips are neutral outlines by spec and the region's job is answering, not committing.
  • Lime. Appears only as Orbit's mark on the avatar. Never a button, never a status.
  • Status without hue. Counts, brightness steps, check icon and words carry every state. No red or green anywhere.
  • Type. Locked scale only, floor at 13px eyebrow: --type-body titles, --type-meta details, --type-label tallies and chips, --type-eyebrow kind and group labels.
  • Growth. Rows are flex columns, chips wrap, tally and metadata wrap with trailing dots so a wrapped line never starts on a separator. Nothing is height-capped; the panel grows and scrolls internally past the composer.
  • Quiet by default. No badge, no pulse, no colour shift on new items. The count changing is the whole notification.
