Four items. Three are missing screens or states; the fourth is a finish-layer pilot on the group home, drawn against the plain version. Dark-first, existing type scale, teal for actions that matter, lime for Orbit alone, status by brightness plus label, no animation anywhere.

Item 01

The front door

What a visitor with no session sees at /. It says what Orbit is, why the thing you create is a group rather than an event, and offers one action. Anyone already in a group is routed straight past it, so there is no returning-user state and no second screen behind this one.

What carries the pitch

Four elements: Orbit's mark, the headline, one lede, one action. The mark sits at 104px in the top corner and the copy sits low, so the screen opens as a statement rather than a splash and the reading order runs down into the button.

  • Headline --type-display 28px, Hanken 800, tight leading, text-wrap: balance. Lede --type-body at --text-secondary, three lines.
  • Bottom-anchored. The copy block takes margin-top: auto, so the mark holds the top, the copy and action group at the bottom, and the space between them absorbs longer translations.
  • One teal action, full width, 52px minimum. Nothing else on this screen is teal.
  • The invite note is 13px --text-faint text, deliberately not a second button: people with a link arrive on the join screen, not here.
  • No nav, no footer, no scroll. The whole pitch fits one screen at default text size and reflows down the page at larger sizes.

A · three cards, first card showing

The snapped card sits at the 16px gutter. The next card's own border and radius show as a 22px sliver against the screen edge, at full strength: dimming or fading it would read as disabled rather than as next.

  • Track spans the full width; its own padding: 0 16px holds the gutter, so the row can scroll past the edge instead of stopping inside a padded box.
  • scroll-snap-type: x mandatory, cards scroll-snap-align: start, scroll-padding-left: 16px. Card width calc(100% - 16px) of the padded track.
  • Active dot 17 × 6 pill at --text-primary; inactive 6 × 6 round at --hairline. Width plus brightness, never hue, so the state survives greyscale.
  • Dots are chrome, not a control surface: tapping them is optional, swiping is the interaction. No transition on the state change.

Before · what overshot

Staying junior was right; the execution read as attachment. The strip is inset 16px, which is exactly the event card's own left and right edges, sits 2px below it, and shares its background. On first open the eye groups it with the card and reads a four-line card with a footer.

  • Matching gutters is the strongest grouping cue on the screen, stronger than the hairline that was meant to divide them.
  • The top hairline lands where a card's internal divider would land, so it reads as a rule inside an object rather than between two.
  • It is also inconsistent with itself: the expanded panel is already full-bleed, so opening the strip changes its width.

After · a band, not a footer

Three changes, none of them a promotion. The strip is still flat, still one line, still hairline-ruled, and still not a card.

  • Full bleed. Rules run edge to edge, margin: 0, so the strip no longer traces the card's silhouette. Nothing on this screen is card-shaped and full-width at once, so the band cannot be mistaken for part of an object.
  • Feed gutter, not card gutter. Contents sit at 20px, aligned with the chat below it, which places the strip with its lower neighbour rather than its upper one. Alignment does the grouping that the hairline could not.
  • Air above. The card region's bottom padding goes 2px to 14px. The gap above the strip is now larger than any gap inside the card, so the card closes before the strip begins.
  • Separation from the feed below is carried by the bottom rule plus the feed's own centred day divider, which is unmistakably feed furniture.
  • Unchanged: one line collapsed, clock icon and chevron, counts as the only bright text, no badge, no pulse, no colour change, absent entirely when nothing is pending. Expanding still overlays the feed; the panel's internals are as drawn.
  • This frame shows the band colourless. The teal wash in 03b sits on exactly this geometry and changes nothing else.

How the wash stays quiet

Both are background-color only: teal at low alpha over --surface-base, so the band lands at roughly #1d2931 (A) and #23343d (B). Against a solid --action button at #18bccb that is a fraction of the chroma, and the band still sits darker than the card above it.

  • A · 7% reads as a tint you notice second, after the card. B · 13% reads as teal on first glance and is about as far as it can go before it starts competing with the card's button.
  • Unchanged from item 03: full-bleed hairlines top and bottom, 20px feed gutter, one-line height, 14px of air above, clock icon at --text-secondary, chevron at --text-faint. No radius, no border box, no shadow.
  • Counts stay the only bright text in the band: --text-primary weight 700 on the numerals, --text-secondary for the words. The wash does not lighten the label.
  • Greyscale-safe. Both washes reduce to a slightly lighter grey band, and the sentence "2 waiting on you" carries the meaning on its own. Nothing here encodes state in hue.
  • Expanded panel: the wash is on the strip only. The panel keeps --surface-base, so opening does not flood the screen with teal.
  • The card's button is the only solid teal on the screen, and the only teal you can press. The band is a surface, not a button, so it never takes the --action-ink label treatment.

Same markup, same tokens, same measurements. The only difference is the .fx class on the screen root, which turns on one stylesheet of eight surface treatments. No layout moves, no type changes, no motion.

The eight moves

  • 1 · Depth wash. A radial top vignette on the screen background, 340px tall, peaking at 11% light. Grain sits on top of it at 5% so the ramp is dithered and cannot band on an OLED panel.
  • 2 · Surface tones. Three steps instead of one: the card lifted by a 5% top-down white gradient, the strip recessed with a 42% black band, the feed left on --surface-base. Regions now separate without any new borders.
  • 3 · Two-layer card shadow. A 1px contact shadow at 55% plus a soft 30px cast at 68%, so the card reads as sitting on the screen rather than stamped into it. It stays the only shadowed object.
  • 4 · Teal primary. A 22% top-lit gradient and a 34% inner top stroke on the fill. The teal itself is untouched, so contrast against --action-ink is unchanged.
  • 5 · Lime glow. A 9px, 30% lime drop-shadow on Orbit's mark in the header and on every Orbit bubble. Reserved for the mark; it never appears on an action, a status, or a chip.
  • 6 · Eyebrow tracking. Uppercase day dividers open to .2em.
  • 7 · Tabular tallies. Counts in the card status line, the strip and Orbit's gauge line switch to tabular figures, so numbers stop shifting as they update.
  • 8 · Grounded composer. A 34% black rise under the pinned input, so the feed ends rather than sliding under it.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No motion. No transitions, no entrance, no shimmer, no parallax. Every value here is static.
  • No new hue. The wash is a desaturated blue-grey at 11%, not a brand tint. Teal and lime keep their exact token values.
  • No new meaning. Nothing in the finish layer carries status. Brightness and label still do that, and greyscaling the screen loses nothing.
  • No layout change. Not one measurement, padding or type size differs between 04a and 04b, so adopting or declining the layer cannot reflow anything.
  • No promotion of the strip. The recessed band pushes the strip further down the hierarchy, not up. The card is still the only raised, bordered, shadowed object with teal on it.

Judging it: add or remove .fx on the screen root, or drop finish-layer.css from the build. Items 02 and 03 live in round4-base.css and are unaffected either way.

Handoff notes

  • New files. round4/round4-base.css holds items 01, 02, 03, the 03b band washes, and the two moves kept from the finish-layer pilot. round4/finish-layer.css holds the declined pilot, every rule scoped to .fx; it is kept for the record and is not part of the build. Load order: walkthrough.css, pending-surface.css, round4-base.css.
  • The band wash is one line to switch: .tint-a (7%) or .tint-b (13%) on the strip. Ship one, delete the other.
  • No new tokens. Every colour, size and leading comes from the existing set. The finish layer adds only black and white alphas over those tokens, plus one 11% blue-grey wash.
  • Overridden from earlier files. .gh-dots (active state), .gh-rail.peek and .gh-pinned.peek (track and card widths), and .pd-strip via the additive .pd-sep class. Nothing else in the shipped CSS is touched, and the strip's own interim rules stay valid for anything not carrying .pd-sep.
  • Type scale. Front-door copy uses display, body, meta and eyebrow only; the 13px eyebrow floor holds. All sizes stay rem-based, so OS text size still scales them.
  • Colour independence. Dot state is width plus brightness. Card status stays a bright count with a word label. The pending band's teal wash is decoration over a sentence that already carries the meaning; greyscale loses nothing.
  • Growth. The front-door rows, card titles, strip line and tallies all wrap. The carousel caps at three cards; a fourth event never enters the pinned region.
  • Assets. Orbit's mark is the existing avatar component (orbit-mark.js here). All other icons are inline stroked paths in this file: arrow, chevron, clock, check.
  • These files are design references built in HTML, not production code. Port the measurements and the structure into the app's own components.
