Interplanetary Groups · round 6

Proposal on a card, without the tallest-card tax

Round-5 board 05 is rejected: its full proposal band made one card much taller than the rest, and the region renders at the height of its tallest card, so every other card sat over dead space. Three replacements, each keeping one plan on one card, no truncation, no internal scroll. Variant A (the owner's lean, two takes) moves the vote to the detail screen and leaves a one-line notice. Variant B trims the band to its essentials. Variant C never shows two answer rows at once. Board 06 answers the mixed-height question for the whole region. Post-round-5 rulings applied throughout: teal need-labels for the viewer's own move, NEEDS OTHER VOTES copy, question-mark idea titles, names-voice tallies, shipped proposal chip copy.

The card asks one thing at a time

  • Height cost ≈ 25–30px, honestly measured: the question line + chip row run slightly taller than the 44px RSVP pair they replace. The card never carries two answer rows at once, so an open vote costs about one text line, the same residue as take A2 and a third of variant B's.
  • Sequence = the label ladder, made physical: NEEDS YOUR RSVP shows the pair; answering flips the label to NEEDS YOUR VOTE and swaps the slot to the proposal; voting flips it to NEEDS OTHER VOTES and the slot empties (tally stays in chat and detail).
  • Your RSVP stays visible: a "You're in ✓" segment leads the counts line, check plus words, never hue. Changing it lives on the detail screen's You're in / Change band, which already exists.
  • Weakness, stated: while the RSVP is unanswered the proposal is only signalled by the shift in the label ladder and by chat; and swapping content between visits is a between-visits change, though never a height change.
  • No animation: the swap is a state render, nothing moves on screen.

Stretch the shell, anchor the ask

  • Rule 1: the rail keeps flex stretch — every card's border runs the full region height. The idea card's hairline shell now contains its own slack.
  • Rule 2: card interiors are columns and the ask block (.gh-ask: the RSVP pair, or the chips + tally) takes margin-top: auto, so every card's answer row lands on a shared baseline just above the dots. The slack sits between the idea's title and its chips, mid-card, where whitespace reads as calm rather than absence.
  • No content stretches, nothing is height-capped, nothing clips; at enlarged text the tallest card simply sets a taller region.
  • Compounding: variant C keeps every confirmed card within a text line of its siblings, so the stretch this rule absorbs is small; A adds one line; B leans on it hardest.

Rulings carried through every board

  • Need labels: the viewer's own move renders in the action teal (NEEDS YOUR RSVP, NEEDS YOUR VOTE); NEEDS OTHER VOTES, exact copy, stays --text-secondary. The label still vanishes when the card needs nothing.
  • Ladder on a card with an open vote: NEEDS YOUR RSVP → NEEDS YOUR VOTE → NEEDS OTHER VOTES.
  • Pending idea titles carry the question mark: "Beers?".
  • Proposal chips: shipped copy, "8pm works" / "Keep 7pm", grey grammar, never teal.
  • Tallies in the names voice, never counts-only: "Rowan & Devon are in so far · one more makes it happen".
  • Unchanged: existing tokens only; Geist; teal never on chips, never a wash, never decorative; lime is Orbit's alone; status never by hue alone, never red vs green; 13px floor; layout grows and never clips; no animation; one plan, one card.
