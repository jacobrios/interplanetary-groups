Interplanetary Groups · round 5

The card region: honest asks, ideas as cards

Two changes to the group home's top carousel and nothing else. The RSVP pair stops presuming a yes: while unanswered, both options are transparent with the action teal on both borders, so the teal marks the question, not an answer. And the pending strip retires: interest gauges and time-change proposals become cards in the carousel, mingled with confirmed events in date order, clearly subordinate by brightness and structure. Geist everywhere. No animation.

The teal marks the ask, not an answer

  • Both options: transparent fill, 1.5px solid var(--action) border, --text-primary label at weight 600. Visually equal; the pair leans toward neither side.
  • No checkmark until an answer exists. The check is a state marker, not decoration.
  • Label .gh-need: 13px uppercase eyebrow, --text-secondary, top right. It names what the card still needs and disappears once it needs nothing.
  • Title, day and time, venue, counts line, chevron, hairline above the pair: shipped card, untouched.
  • Buttons keep min-width: 10.2em and wrap into a stack when they cannot sit side by side.

Date order, not status order

  • Order: ascending by date, kinds mingled. Fri's idea before Sat's event because Fri comes first, not because it matters more.
  • At a glance: the flat hairline shell against the raised, shadowed confirmed card says maybe vs plan without a single hue. The peeking card is never dimmed or masked.
  • Chrome: shipped Round 4 geometry, verbatim. 342px cards, 22px peek, one 6px dot per card, active dot a 17×6 --text-primary pill.
  • Cap: three cards still. Ideas count toward the cap; overflow lives in the feed as before.

Binding rules, unchanged from the system

  • Teal exactly twice: both borders of an unanswered RSVP pair, the chosen option's fill after. Never on chips, never a wash on any card, never decorative. Lime stays Orbit's mark and is never an action.
  • Status never by hue alone, never red vs green. Checkmarks, labels and brightness carry every state on this board.
  • Labels name what the card still needs and vanish when it needs nothing: NEEDS YOUR RSVP · NEEDS YOUR VOTE · NEEDS MORE VOTES. 13px floor, uppercase, --text-secondary.
  • Nothing below 13px. Titles at heading (confirmed) or body (idea), buttons at label, metadata at meta, eyebrows at the floor.
  • Growth: cards grow with content and never clip; the RSVP pair stacks when it cannot sit side by side; assume enlarged device text.
  • Out of scope: feed, composer, header, empty state, event detail (it inherits the RSVP treatment automatically and carries no label), every other screen. The pending strip and panel retire with this round.
